# NIRVANA
## Privacy-First Smart Women's Safety Network

### Safety that senses. Security that protects.

---

## 📌 About The Project

NIRVANA is a privacy-first smart women's safety platform designed to provide faster and more accessible emergency support.

In critical situations, users may not always have enough time to unlock their phones, open an application, or manually contact someone. NIRVANA aims to simplify emergency response by bringing essential safety features together into one unified platform.

The platform focuses on emergency assistance, trusted contact management, safety monitoring, location-based support, and privacy-conscious data handling.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Women's safety continues to be a major concern, especially during situations where immediate assistance is required.

Traditional safety applications often face limitations such as:

- Dependence on manual SOS activation
- Delay in contacting emergency contacts
- Difficulty accessing safety features during stressful situations
- Limited centralized safety monitoring
- Privacy concerns related to continuous location tracking

There is a need for a smart, accessible, and privacy-conscious safety platform that can help users quickly initiate emergency support when required.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

NIRVANA is designed as a smart safety ecosystem that combines multiple emergency support features into a single platform.

The system allows users to:

- Access emergency controls quickly
- Manage trusted emergency contacts
- Activate SOS alerts
- Monitor their safety status
- Access location-based emergency support
- Track emergency activity
- Maintain greater control over personal safety information

NIRVANA follows a privacy-first approach where sensitive information is handled responsibly and shared only when required within the emergency workflow.

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 🚨 Instant SOS Emergency Alert

Users can quickly activate an SOS alert during an emergency situation.

The emergency workflow can initiate:

- Emergency alert generation
- Safety status update
- Location-based assistance
- Trusted contact notification workflow

---

## 👥 Trusted Contacts

Users can add and manage trusted emergency contacts.

This allows the safety network to identify the people who should be notified during an emergency.

Features include:

- Add trusted contacts
- Manage contact details
- Remove contacts
- Emergency contact accessibility

---

## 📍 Location Support

The platform supports location-based emergency assistance to help identify the user's location during critical situations.

This feature can assist in:

- Emergency location identification
- Location-based support
- Faster response coordination

---

## 🛡️ Safety Dashboard

The NIRVANA dashboard provides users with a centralized view of their safety features.

Users can access:

- Current safety status
- SOS emergency controls
- Trusted contacts
- Emergency activity
- Safety monitoring options

---

## ⚠️ Smart Threat Detection Concept

NIRVANA incorporates a smart monitoring concept that can identify unusual or emergency situations.

For the current prototype, threat detection can be demonstrated through simulation.

The future system can integrate:

- IoT sensors
- Wearable devices
- Smart emergency triggers
- Automated safety monitoring

---

## 📊 Emergency Status Tracking

Users can monitor the progress and status of emergency events through the platform.

This improves transparency and allows users to stay informed about active emergency situations.

---

## 🔒 Privacy-First Approach

Privacy is a core principle of NIRVANA.

The platform focuses on:

- Responsible data handling
- Controlled access to sensitive information
- Trusted contact authorization
- Privacy-conscious emergency workflows
- Minimal unnecessary data collection
